# HUMAN SYSTEMS — Image Generation Prompts
Every block = one asset. Copy the **Prompt** text as one piece into the image tool. Use the same tool for the whole B set so the look stays consistent.
Shared style (already included in each prompt): black-and-white cinematic photograph, one directional light, deep detailed blacks, fine 35mm film grain, editorial, no text, no logos, no colour.

## A. PHOTOGRAPHY — shoot & selection briefs (real photos, no AI)

### HS-001 · Hero · Real portrait
- File: `hs-001-hero-portrait` · Ratio 4:5 vertical · Min 2400 × 3000 px · deliver original JPG/RAW
- Setup: plain light-grey wall (or grey paper backdrop) 1.5–2 m behind you; one window or one large softbox at 45° on your left; nothing on the right (let the shadow fall); camera/phone on tripod at eye level, 2–3 m away, zoom to a 70–85 mm look (no wide-angle).
- Wardrobe: plain black crew-neck or black overshirt, no logos.
- Shots: chest-up, body turned 15°, head and eyes to camera, calm confident closed-mouth expression; take 20+ frames. Keep empty wall space on one side (left in some frames, right in others).
- Delivered in colour is fine; conversion to B&W is done in post. No retouching beyond light clean-up. **Do not use AI to change the face.**

### HS-012 · About — main · Real photo
- File: `hs-012-about-diagram-wall` · 4:5 · Min 2400 × 3000 px
- Setup: same light as HS-001. A wall, window or whiteboard with a real hand-drawn diagram (boxes, arrows, a funnel or the 5 steps Understand → Position → Create → Distribute → Optimize).
- Shot: you standing three-quarter view, marker in hand, looking at the diagram (not at the camera), focused. Diagram partly visible and slightly out of focus.

### HS-013 · About — detail · Real photo

- Shot from above: your hands drawing a simple system diagram on white paper with a black pen, light from top-left, sleeves black, plain dark table. Pen tip sharp.

### HS-009 / HS-010 / HS-011 · Selected work · Client photos (selection brief)

## B. AI-GENERATED CONCEPTUAL IMAGERY

### HS-002 · Perspective opener (before "How I think")
- File: `hs-002-atrium-figure` · Aspect 21:9 (desktop) — also generate a 4:5 version for mobile · Min 3000 px wide
- **Prompt:**
Black-and-white cinematic photograph of a vast brutalist concrete atrium seen from a high angle. Hard late-afternoon sunlight enters through tall narrow openings and cuts the polished floor into sharp geometric bands of light and deep shadow. A single small anonymous figure in a dark coat walks across one band of light in the lower third of the frame, casting a long shadow, scale emphasising how large the space is. Strict symmetry, clean architectural lines, repeating columns, depth fading into darkness. Mood: quiet observation, a human moving through a system. Deep detailed blacks, soft highlights, fine 35mm film grain, shot on a 35mm lens, editorial fine-art photography. Leave the upper-left area calm and empty for typography.
- Negative: no colour, no text, no signs, no logos, no crowd, no people facing camera, no visible face, no futuristic sci-fi, no 3D render look, no HDR, no lens flare, no vignette overload.

### HS-005 · How I think — card 3 · "We spent $2K last month but got no qualified leads"
- File: `hs-005-cracked-hourglass` · 16:10 + 4:3 · Min 2400 px wide
- **Prompt:**
Black-and-white fine-art still-life photograph of a large, precise, elegant hourglass standing on a dark stone surface against a deep black background. The upper bulb is full and sand is falling steadily, but the lower bulb is cracked: a fine diagonal fracture lets every grain escape before it collects, so the lower bulb stays almost empty while a growing cone of sand builds up on the stone beside the hourglass. A single hard spotlight from top-left sculpts the glass edges, the falling stream of sand and the fracture line; everything else falls into shadow. Macro-level clarity on the crack and the escaping sand. Mood: resources poured into a system that cannot hold them. Medium-close shot, 100mm macro lens, deep detailed blacks, fine film grain, museum-quality product photography.
- Negative: no colour, no money, no coins, no banknotes, no charts, no text, no shattered explosion, no fantasy glow, no 3D render look, no hands.
