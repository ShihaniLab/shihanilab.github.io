# Motion guidelines
- Purposeful and restrained: motion explains (reveal, connect, build), never decorates.
- Existing motions kept: hero "THE MARKETER" decode + portrait scan, word reveal on headings, city strip (26s loop), method blocks building on scroll, case rows sliding in, fit-test puzzle snapping, service selector switch.
- Durations 0.4–0.8s, ease-out. No loading screens, no video backgrounds, no scroll-jacking.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` disables transitions and the decode/scan effects.
- Performance first: CSS transforms and opacity only; images are webp, lazy by position.
