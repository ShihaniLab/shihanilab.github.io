# Brand identity — HUMAN SYSTEMS
**Understand humans. Decode systems. Create growth.** · Signature: *Where art meets science. That's marketing.*

- **Soufiane Shihani**: the commercial identity. Brand & Digital Growth Strategist, the person clients hire.
- **ShihaniLabs**: the educational brand, where Soufiane shares what he learns. Mentioned on the site, not used as the seller.
- **HUMAN SYSTEMS**: the creative concept behind the website's look. It isn't a third brand name.

## Philosophy
Marketing is the study of people and the systems around them. Understand motivations, interpret the market, position, create relevant communication, distribute it, learn from performance and improve.

## The 50/50 balance
- **Thinking Engine (50%)**: hairline grid, numbered nodes, annotation labels ("FIG. 03 — The growth loop"), crop marks, diagrams, the 5-step loop. All built in code.
- **Human Behavior Lab (50%)**: black-and-white cinematic photography of people and spaces, the real portrait of Soufiane, perception images (eye, decision corridor, two chairs).
- Rule: every photo carries a Thinking Engine mark (crop marks, figure label, node), and every diagram sits next to a human element.

## Positioning
An independent strategist for business owners who want growth built on understanding, not on random activity. No guaranteed results, no invented metrics, no fake testimonials.

## Creative principles
Bold but controlled · cinematic but professional · intellectual but understandable · artistic but commercially effective.
